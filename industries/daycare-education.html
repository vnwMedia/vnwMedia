Daycare & Education FAQ

Questions behind a stronger enrollment journey.

Every statement, image, policy, and process should be approved by the organization and reflect the real program.

What should a daycare or school website include?+

Programs, ages, schedules, locations, leadership, educators, curriculum, environment, key policies, parent resources, tours or applications, contact information, and frequently asked questions.

How can local SEO support enrollment?+

Accurate location and program information, complete pages, helpful parent content, consistent listings, internal links, and authentic reviews can strengthen discovery for relevant local searches.

What type of photography works best?+

Authentic, approved images of the environment, educators, learning spaces, activities, and community are more useful than generic stock photography. The organization should manage consent and privacy requirements.

Can marketing help fill specific programs?+

Yes. Campaigns and landing pages can focus on priority age groups, locations, schedules, seasons, tours, or enrollment windows when capacity and follow-up are aligned.

How should we use reviews?+

Use a consistent process for requesting authentic feedback, follow organization policy, and protect the privacy of children and families in all responses and published content.

What should we measure?+

Track qualified inquiries, tour requests, applications, calls, program interest, location, source, follow-up, and enrollment outcomes available from the organization.
