Frequently Asked Questions

What contractors usually want to know.

What marketing channels work best for home service companies?+

The strongest mix often includes a conversion-focused website, local SEO, Google Business Profile optimization, reviews, Google Ads, and Local Services Ads. The priority depends on your trade, market, seasonality, capacity, and whether you need immediate demand or long-term organic growth.

How do you help a contractor rank in multiple service areas?+

We start with accurate service coverage and build useful service and location context around places you genuinely serve. That may include strong core service pages, carefully selected area pages, Google Business Profile support, consistent business information, local proof, internal links, and technical SEO.

Do we need a separate page for every service?+

Usually, your highest-priority services deserve dedicated pages when they solve different problems or attract different searches. Each page should explain the service clearly, answer real customer questions, show relevant proof, identify the service area, and provide an obvious next step.

Can you manage Google Ads and Local Services Ads?+

Yes. VNW Media can help plan and manage paid search and landing pages around profitable services, high-intent terms, qualified service areas, call tracking, budget priorities, and lead quality. LSA availability and requirements vary by category and location.

How do reviews support contractor growth?+

Reviews help homeowners judge reliability, workmanship, communication, and professionalism. A consistent review process strengthens conversion, supports local visibility, and gives your website authentic language about the customer experience.

How quickly can contractor marketing produce results?+

Paid campaigns can begin generating traffic after launch, while SEO and local authority generally build over time. Timing depends on competition, the current website, account history, service area, budget, and how quickly the business responds to leads.

What should we measure besides website traffic?+

Track qualified calls, estimate requests, booked appointments, close rate, revenue by lead source, cost per qualified lead, and which services generate the strongest opportunities. Traffic matters only when it contributes to business outcomes.

Can you work with an established contractor or a newer company?+

Yes. Newer companies often need positioning, foundational pages, listings, and early lead flow. Established companies may need stronger service architecture, market expansion, campaign efficiency, reputation systems, or better attribution.
