Service Strategy FAQ

Questions about organizing a contractor website.

This option is best when the breadth and profitability of services should lead the page.

Which contractor services deserve their own pages?+

Priority services usually need dedicated pages when they solve different problems, target different searches, require distinct proof, or generate meaningful business value. The structure should reflect the customer’s decision—not an arbitrary page count.

How detailed should each service page be?+

Detailed enough to answer fit, scope, process, proof, area, and next-step questions without padding. Useful specificity is stronger than repeating keywords or writing toward a predetermined word count.

Can one page target several related services?+

Yes, when the services share the same intent and supporting information. Separate them when customers use different language, require different proof, or need a different conversion path.

Where should reviews and credentials appear?+

Place relevant proof near the claims and actions it supports. A roofing warranty belongs near roofing decisions; an emergency-response review belongs near urgent service information.

How does this structure support SEO and GEO?+

Clear service entities, complete visible answers, semantic headings, internal links, original evidence, accurate local information, and matching structured data make the page easier for people and search systems to understand.

Can VNW Media write and organize all this content?+

Yes. We can interview the team, map services, identify missing proof, write the pages, plan internal links, and recommend the photography or project information needed.
