Automotive Marketing FAQ

Built around real driver behavior.

Answers for dealerships, collision centers, and automotive service businesses.

What should an auto body or repair website emphasize?+

Priority services, certifications, insurance or repair process guidance, location, hours, reviews, warranties, team experience, real work, phone action, and an easy estimate or appointment path.

How should dealership marketing differ from repair marketing?+

Dealership marketing usually centers on inventory discovery, vehicle details, financing context, trade-ins, and lead response. Repair marketing is more local, service-specific, reputation-led, and often urgent.

Can SEO support multiple repair services?+

Yes. Complete pages for priority services can answer distinct customer questions and improve internal clarity. The structure should reflect real capabilities rather than creating thin pages for every keyword variation.

Should we use Google Ads?+

Paid search can capture high-intent service or inventory demand when targeting, landing pages, territories, budgets, calls, forms, and lead quality are actively managed.

How important are reviews?+

Reviews are a major trust signal, especially when the customer is concerned about repair quality, price, safety, or dealership credibility. A consistent and authentic request process should be part of the customer journey.

What should we measure?+

Track qualified calls, estimate requests, appointments, inventory inquiries, service type, market, source, response time, and closed or booked outcomes when the business can provide them.
