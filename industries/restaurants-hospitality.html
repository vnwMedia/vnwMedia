Restaurant Marketing FAQ

Questions behind a stronger guest pipeline.

A focused plan should support demand without creating operational promises the business cannot keep.

What should a restaurant website include?+

A mobile-friendly menu, hours, address, directions, reservations or ordering, contact details, atmosphere, dietary or accessibility information where relevant, events, private dining, and clear links to official profiles.

How important is local SEO for restaurants?+

Local discovery is critical because guests frequently search by cuisine, occasion, neighborhood, proximity, or current availability. Accurate listings, strong pages, reviews, and consistent information support that journey.

Should the menu be a PDF?+

A downloadable PDF can be useful, but the primary menu should usually be readable and navigable on the website so mobile guests and search systems can understand it without extra friction.

What content should we post on social media?+

Use a sustainable mix of signature items, preparation, people, atmosphere, events, seasonal moments, guest occasions, and useful updates. The content should reflect the real experience.

Can paid ads help reservations and events?+

Yes. Focused campaigns can support high-value occasions, private events, catering, seasonal menus, openings, and local awareness when targeting, offer, destination, and measurement are aligned.

What should we measure?+

Track reservation actions, calls, directions, online orders, event inquiries, menu engagement, email growth, campaign source, repeat response, and revenue signals available from connected platforms.
