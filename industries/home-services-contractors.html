Contractor FAQs

Questions before the next job campaign.

Practical answers for contractors, trades, and home service companies.

What should a contractor landing page include?+

Service information, service areas, calls and estimate forms, reviews, proof of work, licenses or certifications, process details, warranties, financing if relevant, and clear contact options.

How can local SEO help home service companies?+

Strong service pages, accurate locations and service areas, Google Business Profile activity, reviews, internal links, and helpful content can improve visibility for high-intent local searches.

Should every service have its own page?+

Usually yes when customers search for that service separately. Dedicated pages help match intent, explain the offer, and track which services generate calls or forms.

Can paid ads and SEO work together?+

Yes. Paid ads can capture urgent demand while SEO builds durable visibility. Both should send visitors to pages that match the exact problem and next action.

What should we measure?+

Qualified calls, estimate requests, booked jobs, service area performance, source, cost per lead, close rate, and revenue when that data is available.
