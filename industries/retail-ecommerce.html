Retail & eCommerce FAQs

Questions before the commerce roadmap.

Practical answers for retail brands, eCommerce stores, and product-led businesses.

Do we need a complete store redesign?+

Not always. We first identify friction across acquisition, navigation, product discovery, product detail, cart, checkout, mobile usability, retention, and measurement.

How can product pages convert better?+

Clarify the product value, fit, use, variants, imagery, price, delivery, returns, reviews, questions, comparisons, availability, and next action without overwhelming the shopper.

Can SEO work for a large catalog?+

Yes, but it requires deliberate taxonomy, crawl control, unique category and product information, internal links, structured data, performance, inventory handling, and technical coordination with the platform.

How should paid media and landing pages work together?+

Campaign audiences and promises should lead to relevant collections, products, offers, or campaign pages with consistent creative, clear merchandising, useful proof, and complete measurement.

What eCommerce metrics matter most?+

Useful measures include qualified traffic, product discovery, add-to-cart rate, checkout completion, conversion rate, acquisition cost, average order value, repeat purchase, and customer lifetime value where reliable.
