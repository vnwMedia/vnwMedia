Growth & Measurement FAQ

Questions about marketing accountability.

This option is for contractors who want the page to lead with business performance and decision quality.

What is a qualified contractor lead?+

A qualified lead generally matches the service offered, genuine coverage area, customer type, timing, and minimum opportunity requirements the business establishes.

Can VNW Media track booked revenue?+

We can connect marketing sources to calls and forms and help plan a practical handoff into CRM or job data. Revenue reporting depends on the accuracy and availability of the client’s operational data.

Why can lead volume rise while profitability falls?+

Campaigns may attract low-value work, poor geographic fit, price shoppers, unqualified requests, or demand the team cannot serve efficiently. Volume must be evaluated with quality and outcome.

How should budgets be divided by service?+

Use demand, competition, job value, margin, capacity, seasonality, close rate, and strategic importance. Equal distribution rarely reflects business reality.

What should a monthly contractor report contain?+

Include qualified calls and forms, channel and campaign source, service requested, location fit, cost per opportunity, conversion trends, and specific actions being taken next.

How long should a contractor test a campaign?+

The test window depends on demand volume, budget, sales cycle, seasonality, and data quality. Decisions should use enough qualified information to distinguish a real pattern from a short-term fluctuation.
