Ask a question ↗
Is ad spend included in management fees?+

Media spend and management fees are separate unless a proposal explicitly says otherwise. We agree on budgets and scope before launch.

Can campaigns target only the customers we serve?+

We can configure geography, schedules, search themes, and exclusions around your real capacity, then review the traffic and inquiries that follow.

Do you guarantee a cost per lead?+

No. Competition, offer, page experience, and follow-up affect cost and lead quality. We set tracking and make evidence-based adjustments.
