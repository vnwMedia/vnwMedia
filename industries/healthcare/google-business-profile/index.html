Does every business qualify for a Google Business Profile?+

No. Eligibility depends on the business model and Google’s current rules. We review the real operation before recommending a profile.

Can you manage several locations?+

Yes, where each location is genuine and eligible. Its address, hours, categories, and contact information should reflect actual operations.

Will a profile guarantee map visibility?+

No. A complete, accurate profile is helpful, but local results depend on multiple factors and can change.
