Daycare & Education FAQs

Questions before the enrollment roadmap.

Practical answers for childcare, preschool, school, tutoring, and education businesses.

What should a daycare or school website include?+

Programs, ages, schedules, locations, educators, curriculum, environment, key policies, tours or applications, contact information, and frequently asked questions.

How can local SEO support enrollment?+

Accurate location and program information, complete pages, helpful parent content, consistent listings, internal links, and authentic reviews can strengthen discovery for relevant local searches.

What type of photography works best?+

Authentic, approved images of the environment, educators, learning spaces, activities, and community are more useful than generic stock photography.

Can marketing help fill specific programs?+

Yes. Campaigns and landing pages can focus on priority age groups, locations, schedules, seasons, tours, or enrollment windows when capacity and follow-up are aligned.

What should we measure?+

Qualified inquiries, tour requests, applications, calls, program interest, location, source, follow-up, and enrollment outcomes available from the organization.
