Restaurant FAQs

Questions before the guest campaign.

Practical answers for restaurants, caterers, venues, and hospitality brands.

What should a restaurant landing page include?+

Strong photos, menu access, hours, location, reservation or ordering actions, reviews, private event or catering information, social links, and clear contact details.

How can restaurants improve local SEO?+

Build useful menu and service content, keep business information accurate, strengthen Google Business Profile activity, earn authentic reviews, and publish pages for catering, events, delivery, or priority categories when relevant.

Should online ordering and reservations be prominent?+

Yes. The primary guest action should be visible in the hero and repeated through the page, especially on mobile.

Can social media content support the website?+

Yes. Social content can reinforce specials, events, seasonal dishes, ambience, guest moments, and reputation, while the website gives that demand a reliable conversion path.

What should hospitality businesses measure?+

Reservations, calls, direction clicks, online orders, catering inquiries, event leads, menu views, campaign source, email signups, and repeat engagement.
