Real Estate FAQs

Questions before the market growth plan.

Practical answers for real estate, property, development, and management businesses.

What should a real estate website include?+

Positioning, listings or service paths, communities, team proof, reviews, market resources, contact actions, valuation or consultation options, and follow-up opportunities.

How can local SEO support real estate growth?+

Neighborhood pages, market guides, service pages, listings, internal links, and useful local content can support searches for agents, properties, communities, and real estate services.

Should seller and buyer content be separate?+

Often yes. Sellers and buyers ask different questions and need different proof, language, calls to action, and follow-up.

Can campaigns support listings or developments?+

Yes. Focused landing pages and paid campaigns can support specific listings, communities, developments, seller campaigns, rental demand, or property services.

What should we measure?+

Qualified inquiries, showing requests, valuation requests, lead source, property interest, location interest, email engagement, cost per lead, and closed opportunities when available.
