COMMON PROMPT
Use case: product-mockup.
Create a superb, design-studio quality presentation of a NEW front and back business card for VNW MEDIA, a web design and digital marketing agency.
Input 1: current business card, supplied only to establish company identity and what must be improved on. Do NOT replicate this dated layout, divider rule, icons or giant QR.
Input 2: original transparent logo, supporting brand asset. Preserve the exact elegant thin geometric VNW MEDIA lettering, white VNW/cyan MEDIA and its single small tagline "A Web & Digital Marketing Company". Keep the full logo readable on a dark patch. Do not invent a replacement logo.
The previous concepts were rejected as bland. Make this a distinctive coherent visual identity application with a memorable graphic idea and superb print-design craft, editorial restraint, asymmetry, deliberate hierarchy. Carry navy #061923, electric cyan #00aeef, white/ivory, with Inter-like contact typography. Fraunces-like serif may appear only as specified. No generic web-template-looking design, no brochure of services, no giant marketing headline.
FORMAT: a landscape 1536x1024 premium design presentation on a uniform warm off-white neutral background. Show TWO flat rectangular horizontal business cards, front at upper left, back at lower right, staggered but fully visible without overlap, both large, straight top-down orthographic, each exactly US 3.5:2 ratio, ample comfortable margins. Each card around 57% canvas width, no dramatic perspective. Real matte thick paper, subtle studio shadow and fine paper texture, crisp print sharpness. The front is brand-led; back is functional.
Back contact details (use these fake details exactly): "Jordan Taylor"; "Brand Strategist"; "hello@example.com"; "(201) 555-0148"; "vnwmedia.com". Small services line "WEB / SEARCH / SOCIAL". Put a small tidy black and white QR-like square with a clear white margin, roughly 15% card width, away from other text, with tiny label "LET'S CONNECT". QR is a visual placeholder only. Details must remain legible, aligned, uncluttered. No multicolor contact icons.
Presentation label at very top outside the cards: specified concept number and name in small sober uppercase black type. Very small bottom margin note "DESIGN CONCEPT / SAMPLE DETAILS". Nothing else outside the cards.
Do not copy private personal details from the reference. No red trim lines. No duplicate taglines. No stock photos. No laptop/browser/window icons. No generic arrow-on-solid-rectangle templates. Never use the default centered-logo-only layout. Do not hide a weak design behind lighting or props.


OPTION 1: Facet
Concept label "01 / FACET". The memorable graphic is an enormous sharply folded CYAN V/W-inspired geometric ribbon, two triangular planes in closely related cyan tones, going boldly off the right and upper edges of a nearly black navy front. Its shape is taken from the angles of the existing logo, not a new logo. Design looks like custom Swiss graphic design in two-color ink, not an illustration of a 3D object. Compose the original full logo at lower left occupying 53% width, confidently sized and given breathing room; graphic occupies the opposite half and does not touch the logo. Tiny vnwmedia.com at upper left. Back: warm white, a large single diagonal cyan slash wrapping over the left edge like a continuation of the front; content inset well clear of the graphic; Jordan Taylor dark navy 22pt, Brand Strategist 9pt; disciplined contact stack bottom-left; small QR bottom-right. A tiny navy/cyan brand lockup top left on a very slim dark panel if needed. No horizontal divider rule. Cyan edge-color on both thick cards visible only subtly. Bold, distinctive, professionally restrained.

OPTION 2: Signal
Concept label "02 / SIGNAL". A bespoke kinetic moire wave pattern is the brand graphic: 18-24 clean cyan and navy contour lines bend smoothly through a large sweeping S-curve, like a signal spreading, cropped forcefully across the right two-thirds of a deep midnight navy front. Vary spacing to create a sense of motion without a cheesy glow. Large black negative-space ellipse/area at lower left cleanly holds the exact original full logo. This is sophisticated contemporary cultural-studio print design, abstract generative graphic, not telecommunications iconography. Reverse uses the same navy background, but minimal: Jordan Taylor in beautiful larger ivory Fraunces-style serif on top left, role below in crisp small sans, contact stack bottom-left, compact QR in white square bottom-right; one cropped cyan curve on far right outer edge carries continuity. Navy and cyan with generous negative space. Create a visible tactile spot-gloss finish ONLY on a few navy pattern lines, subtle and plausible, not neon glow. No slogan.

OPTION 3: Sculpt
Concept label "03 / SCULPT". Custom sculptural graphic: an elegant continuous folded ribbon forming an abstract W loop, electric blue outer face and brushed liquid-silver inner face, exquisitely lit, sharply cut and dimensional, like a collectible piece of contemporary design. It occupies the RIGHT half of a white/very pale grey front, boldly cropped by the right card edge. A compact deep navy rectangular area spans just the left half, with the exact full white/cyan original logo elegantly placed centered vertically in it; intentional hard-edge split composition, no gradients background. Small "WEB / SEARCH / SOCIAL" sits below original logo separated by ample space. The sculpture is printed artwork on the card, the paper remains flat matte. Reverse completely midnight navy: large white name top-left; cyan small role beneath; perfectly aligned contact info bottom-left, QR white inset bottom-right. Tiny fragment of sculptural ribbon in upper-right corner of reverse only, fully separate from QR. Very crisp experimental agency art direction, not futuristic gaming or crypto. No slogan.

OPTION 4: Aperture
Concept label "04 / APERTURE". A premium specialty-print concept using one distinctive small physical triangular die-cut aperture at the upper-right corner area, a triangle echoing the A in MEDIA, roughly 12% card width, inset enough that all four card outer edges stay rectangular and sturdy. The aperture appears as an actual hole showing the background. Front deep matte midnight navy with an oversized subtle tonal varnish V/W angular line motif filling the bottom half, full exact original logo aligned left at the upper-left/middle, generous optical whitespace. Cyan paper core visible at the small die-cut hole and at card edge. Back is FULL CYAN with dark navy text. A strict editorial vertical split: name and role top-left, contact info bottom-left, small QR at lower-right. The triangular aperture in the back is mirrored upper-left (because flipping the physical card), leaving intentional margin and content offset to avoid it. A short Fraunces italic word "Connect." in small dark type toward top-right. The genius is the silhouette detail, typography and quiet material quality. Absolutely no big diagonal color panels, no plastic, no transparent acrylic, no gadgets, no busy decoration.

OPTION 5: Overscale
Concept label "05 / OVERSCALE". Front is a confident white field with extremely oversized custom geometric outline letters VNW, clearly derived from the angular VNW part of the original mark, spanning the entire card and cropped by top, left and right edges. These three decorative outline letters are drawn with crisp navy strokes, very large and architectonic; one single central diagonal stroke is solid electric cyan creating a graphic accent. This is bespoke typography-as-image and should feel like a respected design studio, not a giant generic sans slogan. The exact complete original VNW MEDIA logo with tagline is preserved separately at normal readable size within a slim full-width navy strip along the bottom of the front; vnwmedia.com tiny at right of strip. Reverse an asymmetric white and navy composition: a narrow 20%-width navy vertical strip at LEFT with small vertically oriented "VNW MEDIA" white; the main white 80% region has elegant dark serif Jordan Taylor over two lines in upper-left of its space, smaller sans role below, two tidy contact lines at bottom left, small QR bottom-right, one vivid cyan small square near the name as the repeated graphic signal. Strong negative space, typographic confidence, no giant marketing copy. Crisp flat ink with no foil or gradients.
