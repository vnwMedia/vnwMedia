Ask about your case study ↗
Why add detailed case study pages?+

Detail pages let VNW explain the problem, strategy, services, creative direction, SEO thinking, and conversion path behind the work instead of only showing screenshots.

Should each case study include metrics?+

Use real metrics when available. If analytics are not confirmed, it is better to show strategy, deliverables, trust factors, and conversion logic instead of inventing numbers.

How does this help SEO?+

Case studies can naturally include service keywords, industry language, FAQs, internal links, location relevance, and helpful explanations of web design and digital marketing decisions.

Which detail layout is best?+

The immersive story is strongest for flagship work, the dashboard is strongest for lead generation, the editorial layout is warmest, and the deck layout is best for fast executive scanning.
