Ask for clarity about the relationship

Request a written scope that explains the services, responsibilities, deliverables, and review schedule. Ask what information you need to provide and how changes outside the agreed scope are handled. Confirm who owns the accounts and creative work, and what happens if you decide to move to another provider.

Ask to see an example report and have the agency explain how it leads to a decision. Look for a clear distinction between activity, leads, and business outcomes. Agree on a primary contact and a process for questions, approvals, and urgent website or campaign issues.

Marketing outcomes depend on the market, budget, implementation, and customer experience. Rankings, lead volume, and revenue are not guaranteed.
