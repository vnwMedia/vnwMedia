Write a website brief before requesting a quote

Describe your customers, primary services, current website problems, and the actions a visitor should complete. For an online store, add the product catalog, payment requirements, shipping rules, inventory process, and who will maintain the information. Share the same brief with every potential development partner.

Ask what is included in design, copy, migration, testing, and ongoing support. Confirm who controls the domain, hosting, analytics, and website accounts. Before launch, test the complete inquiry or purchase journey on a phone and desktop, including confirmation messages and the internal notification.

Marketing outcomes depend on the market, budget, implementation, and customer experience. Rankings, lead volume, and revenue are not guaranteed.
