Connect marketing and customer follow-up

Map the steps from discovering your business to receiving a response. Include the ad or search listing, website page, form or phone call, confirmation, and staff follow-up. Identify who owns each step and where information is currently lost or entered twice.

Review a small sample of real inquiries with the team that handles them. Ask whether the person was looking for the right service, whether contact details were usable, and whether the response answered the original question. Use those observations to improve both marketing messages and the internal process.

Marketing outcomes depend on the market, budget, implementation, and customer experience. Rankings, lead volume, and revenue are not guaranteed.
