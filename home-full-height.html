Homepage Option FAQ

Questions before choosing this direction.

This variant is built for a more dramatic landing experience while keeping conversion paths clear.

Discuss this homepage ↗
What changed in this homepage version?+

The hero/banner fills the full browser height, and the Services section is also a full browser-height section with its own internal scrollbar.

Why make the banner 100% browser height?+

It creates a stronger first impression and makes the main message, proof, and CTA feel more premium and focused.

Why make Services a full-height scroll section?+

It keeps the services area contained, interactive, and easier to browse without making the homepage feel endlessly long.

Does this still work on mobile?+

Yes. On smaller screens the layout relaxes into a natural stacked flow so the section remains usable.

Should this replace the current homepage?+

This is a strong alternate direction if you want a more immersive homepage. The current homepage remains available as the safer long-form layout.
