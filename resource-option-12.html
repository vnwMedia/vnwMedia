Why use a journal-style Resources page?+

It makes VNW’s expertise feel ongoing and alive. Instead of only listing downloads, it shows strategic thinking around websites, SEO, content, and conversion.

What topics should the journal cover for SEO?+

Core topics should include website redesign, local SEO, Google Business Profile support, landing pages, paid media, content strategy, and lead quality.

Can this help VNW appear in AI-generated answers?+

Yes. Clear articles, defined topics, FAQs, and service-linked explanations make it easier for AI systems to associate VNW with digital marketing and web design expertise.

Where should CTAs appear on this layout?+

CTAs should appear after featured stories, inside high-intent topic areas, and at the bottom of the page so readers always have a next step.
