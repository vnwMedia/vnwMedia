Why use an editorial dispatch format?+

It gives VNW a stronger point of view. Instead of a generic blog grid, the page feels curated, strategic, and authored by experts.

Can this still support SEO?+

Yes. The departments create topic clusters around web design, local SEO, Google Business Profile, lead generation, and AI-ready content.

How does this support GEO visibility?+

The editorial format includes clear explanations, FAQs, definitions, and service connections that make the content easier for AI answer systems to parse.

Where do CTAs belong?+

CTAs appear after the cover story, inside department paths, and at the end of the page once the reader understands the value.
