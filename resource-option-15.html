Showroom FAQs

Keep the visual option search-friendly.

FAQs give this more image-forward layout the explanatory depth it needs for SEO, Google visibility, and AI search.

Can a visual Resources page still rank well?+

Yes, if the page includes descriptive headings, crawlable text, internal links, FAQs, and supporting sections that explain the topics behind the visuals.

What topics should the showroom highlight?+

It should highlight website redesign, local SEO, Google Business Profile support, landing-page conversion, content strategy, and digital marketing audits.

How does this support GEO visibility?+

The page pairs visual cards with answer-focused copy, making it easier for AI search tools to understand the relationship between VNW’s services and common business problems.

Where should CTAs be placed in this design?+

CTAs should appear after the guide cards, in the trust section, and at the final banner so visitors always have a way to request help.
