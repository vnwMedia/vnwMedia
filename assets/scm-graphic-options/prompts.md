Composition: Compose exactly TWO devices: a large slim desktop monitor in three-quarter view dominating the left-center, plus one upright smartphone overlapping its lower right. The desktop shows the supplied SC Motors website with the building hero and a concise visible trust/service section below. The phone shows the mobile version with a prominent contact action. Subtle cyan light glow behind, white seamless studio floor with soft grounded shadows. Confident asymmetric two-device sculpture, sophisticated restrained realism. No tablet or laptop, no original four-device stack.
