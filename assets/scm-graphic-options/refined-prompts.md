# SC Motors — refined graphic concepts

Created with the built-in image-generation tool, based on the selected location collage and realistic desk mockup. These are composition concepts, not exact website screenshots. The live case-study page is unchanged.

## 03A — The Location Halo

Use case: compositing.
Asset type: alternative premium website case-study graphic, landscape 4:3, a polished raster illustration of a real website on realistic devices.
Input references: image 1 is the user's chosen location-photo-circle plus laptop/phone composition. Image 2 is the user's chosen realistic white-desk laptop/phone photography. Image 3 is the ORIGINAL SC Motors website screenshot reference, authoritative for branding and screen content. Image 4 is the actual SC Motors Collision Center building photograph, authoritative for facade/signage.
Primary request: create a fresh alternative that stays close to the two selected compositions, combining the real location and a photorealistic laptop and smartphone showing its website. This is a presentation composition, not a website redesign.
Brand/content invariants: preserve the actual SC MOTORS / COLLISION CENTER building sign and existing website car-outline logo as faithfully as possible. Keep facade architecture recognizable. Website content must be SC Motors auto/collision repair, not car sales. Use short readable screen labels "Services", "Contact Us", "Request an Estimate". No invented addresses, phone numbers, reviews, metrics, awards or performance claims; OMIT the tiny address strip rather than fabricating text. Keep tiny nonessential text understated. No option labels, titles or captions outside devices.
Art direction: predominantly WHITE edges/background so it integrates into a white webpage section. Photorealistic brushed-aluminum laptop and graphite smartphone, authentic screen reflections without obscuring content, realistic perspective, soft grounded shadows, restrained cyan #00aeef trim. Modern editorial agency feel. Avoid blueprint grids, diagrams, holograms, cartoon icons, visual clutter, oversized borders, heavy black backgrounds, extra devices, people, office props and watermarks. Devices fill substantial frame area, not tiny thumbnails.
Composition: A refined evolution of reference 1. Place a broad circular crop of the exact building photo behind and slightly RIGHT of center, with the sign plainly visible in the upper half. In foreground a realistic open silver laptop spans the lower LEFT/center at a gentle three-quarter angle; a standing smartphone overlaps at lower RIGHT. Laptop shows actual building hero and service/contact content; phone shows mobile contact view. Keep just one fine cyan crescent tracing part of photo circle. Large devices, soft white studio floor, balanced airy margins. Swap the visual hierarchy and placement from the original while preserving its clean circular-photography language.
