# Homepage hero image study — prompt set

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool for five alternative VNW Media homepage hero backgrounds. The existing homepage is unchanged.

## Option 01 — Partner conversation

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: VNW Media homepage hero background image, used behind white headline on the left and a light contact form on the right. Create a premium editorial photograph of a small-business owner and a senior digital strategist working together at a table in a real, contemporary local business workspace. They discuss a tablet showing an abstract website layout; warm human connection, attentive expressions, natural posture, not looking at camera. Wide cinematic composition, people and tablet mostly in the middle third, darker low-detail negative space toward both edges for webpage overlays. New Jersey small-business feeling without geographic landmarks or signs. Soft window light with restrained cool blue accents, authentic fabric/wood/glass texture, sophisticated documentary photography. No logos, no readable text, no watermarks, no exaggerated glowing graphics. Do not render the webpage headline, buttons, or form inside the image.

## Option 02 — Digital craft

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: VNW Media homepage hero background image, used behind white headline on the left and a light contact form on the right. Create a striking close editorial photograph inside a modern creative studio: a designer's hands refining a website experience on a large desktop display while a second hand marks a paper wireframe nearby. Show recognizable craft and thoughtful detail, but screen content must be abstract layouts without legible words, fake metrics, or logos. Camera from a slightly elevated over-the-shoulder angle, cinematic wide composition with the core visual in the center and quiet darker space at left and right for webpage overlays. Restrained charcoal, warm neutral, and cyan-blue accents; realistic reflected screen light, tactile desk materials, no stock-photo polish. No text, no watermarks, no floating graphics. Do not include the site's headline, buttons, or form in the image.

## Option 03 — Local business

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: VNW Media homepage hero background behind website headline at left and white contact form at right. A cinematic editorial photograph of a thriving independent neighborhood business at blue hour: warmly lit storefront interior visible through tall windows, a few real customers and owner in natural motion, quiet urban New Jersey streetscape without specific recognizable location. Convey the real-world businesses that benefit from digital visibility, not generic technology. Wide panoramic establishing composition with meaningful scene concentrated across the middle band; leave subdued low-detail darker areas toward left and right for overlays. Realistic reflections on pavement and glass, muted charcoal and warm amber with a subtle cyan/blue accent. No readable business sign, no logos, no billboards, no legible text, no watermark, no fake UI, no webpage elements.

## Option 04 — Strategy workshop

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: VNW Media homepage hero background behind white headline at left and light form at right. A premium documentary photograph of a digital agency workshop in progress viewed from a few steps back: two creative professionals standing at a large wall of website wireframes, customer-journey sketches and layout printouts, one pointing at a flow while the other listens. Human scale and collaborative energy, not posed at camera. Wide cinematic frame, the active wall and people in the middle distance, soft darker low-detail negative space at both outer thirds for web overlays. Warm architectural light mixed with subtle cool-blue monitor glow, charcoal and natural paper tones, authentic paper and wall texture. Any marks on sketches must be abstract and unreadable. No legible words, no brand logo, no watermark, no overlaid website typography.

## Option 05 — Connected systems

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: VNW Media homepage hero background behind white headline on the left and a light contact form on the right. Create a sophisticated, restrained editorial visual metaphor for connected digital growth: an architectural dark glass-and-metal workspace seen in perspective, with a few thin luminous cyan pathways linking small points of light across layered transparent planes, suggesting website, search, and campaigns working as one system. Ground the scene in physical materials and photographic lighting rather than a flat infographic or a sci-fi HUD. Wide cinematic composition; primary visual movement near center, calm darker negative space on both sides for webpage overlays. Charcoal, ink black, deep slate and VNW-style electric cyan, subtle reflected highlights. No symbols, numbers, text, dashboards, logos, icons, watermarks, neon overload, or website UI.
